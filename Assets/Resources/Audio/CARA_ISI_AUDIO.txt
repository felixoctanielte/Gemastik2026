ISI FILE AUDIO DI SINI
======================
Cara 1 (paling mudah):
  Unity menu: PeduliTransit → Create Audio Library Asset
  Lalu drag AudioClip ke slot BGM/SFX di Inspector.

Cara 2 (otomatis by nama file):
  Bgm/: Hub.wav, GameplayKrl.wav, GameplayBus.wav, GameplayAngkot.wav, Result.wav
  Sfx/: UiClick.wav, ScorePlus.wav, ScoreMinus.wav, LoudTalking.wav, ... (nama = enum SfxId)

Format: .wav / .ogg / .mp3 (disarankan wav atau ogg).
